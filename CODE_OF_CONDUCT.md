# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive in issues, reviews and discussions.
Disagreement about implementation is welcome; harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexual content directed at participants, and disclosure of
someone else's private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close discussions, or restrict
participation. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and context.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at
security@greatping.com (subject: Community conduct), with links to the relevant public
conversation where possible. Do not post personal information in a public issue.
Security vulnerabilities belong in the private Security reporting channel.
